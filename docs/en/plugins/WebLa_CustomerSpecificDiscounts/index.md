# Customer Specific Discounts

> Assign individual discounts per customer on categories, product groups, or entire customer groups.

## Overview

**Customer Specific Discounts** allows you to assign individual discounts for your customers - whether on specific categories, dynamic product groups, or as a global discount for entire customer groups. The plugin is ideal for B2B shops, loyal customer pricing, and individual pricing strategies.

With the flexible priority system, you can precisely control which discount is applied when multiple discounts could apply to a product. Management is done directly in the customer profile or in the customer group settings in the Shopware admin.

## Key Features

- **Product Discounts**: Set discounts directly on individual products via custom fields
- **Individual Customer Discounts**: Assign discounts per customer on specific categories or dynamic product groups
- **Global Customer Discounts**: Give individual customers a global discount for all products
- **Customer Group Discounts**: Assign discounts for entire customer groups on categories or product groups
- **Global Group Discounts**: Define a flat discount for all products for a customer group
- **Flexible Priority Control**: Determine which discount is applied when there are overlaps
- **Customizable Display**: Choose between separate discount line item or reduced price

## Requirements

- Shopware Version: 6.5.0 to 6.7.x
- PHP Version: 8.1 or higher

## Quick Start

1. Install the plugin via Plugin Manager or Composer
2. Activate the plugin in **Extensions → My Extensions**
3. Configure the basic settings in **Extensions → My Extensions → Customer Specific Discounts → Configure**
4. Open a customer profile and add discounts in the "Customer Discounts" section
