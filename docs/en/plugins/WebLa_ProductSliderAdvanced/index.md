# Product Slider Advanced

> Advanced slider for products and categories in Shopware 6

## Overview

The **Product Slider Advanced** plugin extends your Shopware 6 store with a flexibly configurable slider for products and categories. With this plugin, you can present your products and categories in an appealing modern slider, directing your customers' attention to specific areas of your shop.

The plugin integrates seamlessly into Shopware 6's Shopping Experiences editor and offers extensive design options. You can display not only products but also categories in a slider - ideal for your homepage or landing pages.

A special highlight is the integrated teaser feature: Next to the slider, you can display an attractive teaser area with image, headline, text, and link to place additional information or call-to-actions.

## Key Features

- **Flexible Product Selection**: Select products manually, load them from a category, or use dynamic product groups
- **Category Slider**: Present multiple categories as slider elements with image and description
- **Integrated Teaser**: Optional teaser area next to the slider with image, text, and link
- **Responsive Design**: Optimized display on all devices with configurable mobile behavior
- **Extensive Styling Options**: Customize colors, spacing, heights, and layout individually
- **Variant Control**: Decide whether to show parent products, variants, or both
- **Navigation & Autoplay**: Configurable navigation arrows, dot navigation, and automatic scrolling

## Requirements

- Shopware Version: 6.5.0 or higher (up to 6.7.x)
- PHP Version: As per Shopware requirements

## Quick Start

1. Install the plugin via Plugin Manager or Composer
2. Activate the plugin in **Extensions → My Extensions**
3. Open a Shopping Experience under **Content → Shopping Experiences**
4. Add the **Product Slider Advanced** block from the "Commerce" category
5. Configure the content type and select your products or categories
