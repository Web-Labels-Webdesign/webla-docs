# Images in Product Reviews

> Customers upload images with their product reviews. The gallery renders on the product detail page and as a CMS element in Shopping Experiences.

## Overview

This plugin lets your customers upload their own photos with every product review. Images are stored together with the review and shown as a gallery on the product detail page. The result: authentic user-generated content that builds trust with new buyers.

In addition, the plugin ships its own CMS element so you can freely place review galleries anywhere in your Shopping Experiences — for example, on product detail pages or product listing pages.

All uploaded files are managed through Shopware's standard media library, so your admin team keeps full control over customer content at all times.

## Key Features

- **Image upload in the review form**: Customers can drag & drop or select one or more images while writing a review.
- **Gallery on the product page**: All approved review images appear next to the matching review.
- **`image-reviews` CMS element**: A free-position element for Shopping Experiences of type product page and product listing.
- **Admin review detail**: Uploaded images appear inside the review detail view for moderation.
- **Optional fallback images**: Display a custom image under the "Add new review" button or as a background when no reviews exist yet.
- **Cross-variant gallery**: Optionally show images from reviews of all product variants together.
- **Single codebase for Shopware 6.6 and 6.7**: One ZIP package covers both major versions.

## Requirements

- Shopware 6.6 or 6.7
- PHP 8.2 or higher
- Product reviews enabled in the target sales channel

## Quick Start

1. Install the plugin via the Shopware Store or upload the ZIP.
2. Activate the plugin under **Extensions → My Extensions**.
3. Review basic settings under **Extensions → My Extensions → Images in product reviews → Configure**.
4. Optionally assign fallback images and a media folder.
5. Submit a test review and verify the gallery renders on the product page.
